Privacy Policy · Effective October 22, 2026

QR Scanner Pro processes scans and generates QR codes on your device. The app has no internet permission, advertising, analytics, account system, or developer-operated backend.

Who we are

QR Scanner Pro (Android package com.deploydulupulangnanti.qrcodescanner) is provided by PT Deploy Dulu Pulang Nanti. For privacy questions or support, email seandyadryan2@gmail.com.

Information processed on your device

The app uses camera frames to read QR codes and barcodes. Camera frames are processed in memory and are not recorded, saved as photographs, or uploaded. If you select an image with Android’s photo picker, the app reads that selected image locally to find a code. It does not request access to your entire photo library.

Decoded and generated content can contain personal or sensitive information, such as contact details or Wi-Fi credentials. Up to 1,000 saved codes, their timestamps, format, favorite status, and source (scanned or created) are stored in private app storage. Preferences, including history saving, haptic feedback, and appearance, are also stored locally. We do not receive this information.

Permissions

  • Camera: requested when you enable live scanning. You may refuse or revoke it in Android settings and continue using QR creation or selected-image scanning.
  • Vibration: used for optional haptic feedback after a successful scan.
  • Files and photos: Android’s system picker grants access only to an image or output location you choose. No broad storage permission is requested.

Your controls and retention

You can disable automatic history saving, delete individual codes, or clear all history and favorites in Settings. Disabling automatic saving does not erase existing history. Starring a result explicitly saves it as a favorite even if automatic saving is disabled. New saved codes replace the oldest records once the 1,000-record limit is reached.

App data remains until you delete it, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app. Android cloud backup is disabled. Temporary PNG files prepared for sharing are kept in the app cache and older files are removed on a later image share or when Android clears the cache. Files you export or share outside the app remain at the destination until you remove them there.

Sharing and external services

We do not collect, sell, or transmit your scans to our servers. When you choose to open a website, use a dialer or email app, share content, copy to the clipboard, or export a file, the selected content is passed to that destination or Android service. External apps, websites, keyboards, clipboard features, and storage providers operate under their own policies. Review code contents and destinations before opening or sharing them. The app does not verify the safety of a website.

Purchases and Google Play

If you buy the app through Google Play, Google processes payment and distribution under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive payment card details through this app. Google Play and Android may independently process store, device, or diagnostic information through their platform services.

Support correspondence

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use that correspondence to answer your request and retain it only as needed for support and applicable obligations. Please do not email passwords or sensitive scan contents. You may contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable retention obligations.

Children and international use

The app is a general-purpose utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. Local processing works the same regardless of country. If you contact support, your email may be handled by the email services used by you and us in their operating locations.

Security and policy updates

App-private storage and Android’s permission system help protect local data. Device security and the security of destinations you choose also matter. Future changes to data handling will be reflected in this policy with an updated effective date.
